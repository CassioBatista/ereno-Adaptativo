\paragraph{What the replay can show}
Under retention, the sign of the comparison of Section~\ref{sec:res-switching} follows from the policy,
under two conditions on each round~$r$. Let $A_r$ be the nodes that reach the aggregator and
$\Gamma_r$ the nodes whose links to both overlay neighbours are up, that is, the participants of
fixed FL and of fixed GL in round~$r$ (Section~\ref{sec:impl}), and let $H_i^{P}(r)$ be the set of
specialists held by node~$i$ after round~$r$ under configuration
$P\in\{\mathrm{R},\mathrm{F},\mathrm{G}\}$: ReSIDS, fixed FL and fixed GL.
(C1)~\emph{Mode consistency}: under ReSIDS, node~$i$ federates in round~$r$ if and only if
$i\in A_r$, and gossips if and only if $i\in\Gamma_r\setminus A_r$; it holds when each monitor
trusts a target exactly while the link to it is up and no trust level changes within $S$ before the
round.
(C2)~\emph{Uniform reach}: if $A_r\neq\emptyset$, then $\Gamma_r\subseteq A_r$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:dominance}
If C1 and C2 hold in every round, then
$H_i^{\mathrm{F}}(r)\cup H_i^{\mathrm{G}}(r)\subseteq H_i^{\mathrm{R}}(r)$ for every node~$i$ and
round~$r$. Consequently, in every round, every node flags under ReSIDS every sample that it flags
under fixed FL or under fixed GL.
\end{proposition}

\begin{pf}
The three configurations train the same specialists from the same data, deterministically and in the
same rounds, and every operation of Algorithm~1 is a union with the set already held, so
$H_i^{P}(r-1)\subseteq H_i^{P}(r)$ for every~$P$. Let $\tilde H_i^{P}$ be $H_i^{P}(r-1)$ together
with the specialists that node~$i$ trains in round~$r$. Before the first round, every node holds only
its own specialists under every configuration. Assume the claim after round $r-1$, so that
$\tilde H_i^{\mathrm{F}}\cup\tilde H_i^{\mathrm{G}}\subseteq\tilde H_i^{\mathrm{R}}$ for every~$i$.

If $A_r\neq\emptyset$, then by C1 and C2 ReSIDS federates exactly the nodes of $A_r$, as fixed FL
does, and no node gossips. A node $i\in A_r$ receives $\bigcup_{j\in A_r}\tilde H_j^{\mathrm{R}}$,
which contains $\bigcup_{j\in A_r}\tilde H_j^{\mathrm{F}}=H_i^{\mathrm{F}}(r)$. Under fixed GL,
$i$ keeps $\tilde H_i^{\mathrm{G}}$ or, if it heads the round, adds the sets of its neighbours
in~$\Gamma_r$, which lie in $A_r$ by C2, so that
$H_i^{\mathrm{G}}(r)\subseteq\bigcup_{j\in A_r}\tilde H_j^{\mathrm{G}}\subseteq H_i^{\mathrm{R}}(r)$.
A node $i\notin A_r$ keeps its set under ReSIDS and under fixed FL, and under fixed GL too, since the
head of a gossip round lies in $\Gamma_r\subseteq A_r$.

If $A_r=\emptyset$, fixed FL exchanges nothing, and by C1 ReSIDS gossips over $\Gamma_r$, the
participants of fixed GL. The head is chosen in turn among the participants, so both configurations
have the same head~$h$, which takes the union of its set with those of its neighbours
in~$\Gamma_r$; $H_h^{\mathrm{G}}(r)\subseteq H_h^{\mathrm{R}}(r)$ then follows from the hypothesis
applied to $h$ and to each of them, and every other node keeps its set.

Finally, a node flags a sample when any specialist it holds scores it at least 0.5, so a larger held
set flags a superset of the samples.
\end{pf}

The proposition fixes the sign of the comparison, not its size. Recall on any set of attack types,
and with it the share of nodes protected, is never lower under ReSIDS than under either fixed mode;
nor is the false-positive rate, the cost of retention seen in Section~\ref{sec:res-nodeloss}. What
remains to be measured is how much each fixed mode loses, which depends on how long the aggregator or
the neighbours are lost and on the period of the rounds, and whether C1 and C2 hold on recorded trust
levels. Both hold in every round of the two runs of Disaster-FD, the 82 of T1 and the 97 of T2, with
settle times of 5 and 10\,s. C1 fails where probes are lost, in the rounds in which the mode of a node
differs from that on the recorded run (Section~\ref{sec:fd-interface}), and when the gossip band starts
at 15, so that a node with a single trusted neighbour gossips while fixed GL does not. C2 fails when
the aggregator returns for only part of the nodes that can gossip, as in the variant of T2 constructed
in Section~\ref{sec:res-discussion}. Where either fails, the proposition does not apply, and only the
replay decides.
